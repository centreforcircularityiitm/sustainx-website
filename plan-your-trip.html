Go Green

As a conference dedicated to sustainability, we highly encourage all attendees to utilize public transport. Let's work together to minimize our collective carbon footprint!

hotel
apartment

Accommodation Information

We won't provide any accommodation; participants are requested to arrange for their stay by themselves.

There are many PG hostels and hotels available nearby IIT Madras (Adyar, Velachery, and Taramani areas). We recommend exploring these options early to ensure a comfortable visit.

security

Vehicle Entry Rules

IIT Madras has strict security protocols for vehicle entry. Please set your destination carefully based on your mode of transport.

Main Gate

Allows ALL vehicles (Cabs, Autos, Cars, Two-Wheelers). Entry to ICSR building via cab is only possible through this gate.

Taramani & Velachery Gates

Strictly restricted to Two-Wheelers and Pedestrians ONLY. Cabs and Autos will drop you at the gate.

electric_bolt

Internal Eco-Transit

IIT Madras operates a free fleet of eco-friendly Electric Buses.

Pro Tip: Pay attention to the destination board on the e-bus:

• "Hostel" board: Heading into the campus.
• "Main Gate" board: Heading towards the exit.

Always ask to get down at the ICSR stop.

schedule

The institute bus service operates from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM with a frequency of every 15 minutes.

smartphone

Fair Pricing Alert

First time in Chennai? Do not take direct street-hail autos/cabs. Use apps like Uber, Ola, or Rapido to avoid overcharging (rates can be 2-3x higher otherwise).

info

Terminus Alert

Most outstation buses now terminate at KCBT Kilambakkam. Some still use CMBT Koyambedu. Verify your terminus before travel.

A From Kilambakkam (KCBT)

Train from Urapakkam to Guindy Station.

Cross to Race Course stop, take bus 3, 51A, or 119.

Direct Bus

Take 21G directly to IIT Main Gate.

B From Koyambedu (CMBT)

Take Metro to Guindy Station (via Alandur).

Cross overbridge to Race Course stop, take bus 3, 51A, or 119.

Enter Velachery Gate and take the internal e-bus.

pets
warning

Wildlife Interaction Protocol

The IIT Madras ecosystem is a protected forest area containing deers and monkeys. To ensure your safety and the well-being of the animals, please adhere to the following:

Spotted Deer

Macaque Interaction Warning

Strict Prohibition

Do not attempt to feed any wildlife. It disrupts their natural diet and behavior.

Personal Safety

Avoid carrying open food or water bottles in your hands. Monkeys are quick and may attempt to take them away.
