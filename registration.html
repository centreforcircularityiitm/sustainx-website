Submission Guidelines

01

Abstract Format

Abstracts must be limited to 250 words, clearly stating the research objective, methodology, key findings, and policy implications. Use standard academic English (UK or US, consistently). Avoid abbreviations in the title.

02

Presentation Modes

Oral Presentation: 15-minute presentation + 5-minute Q&A.
Poster Presentation: A0 size, vertical format, displayed in the ICSR Grand Gallery.

03

Thematic Alignment

All submissions must clearly indicate the primary focal theme (AI, Water, Energy, Battery, or Plastics). Cross-cutting submissions are accepted under the most relevant theme with justification.

04

Review Process

All submissions undergo double-blind peer review by the Scientific Committee. Quality, originality, and relevance to circular economy principles are the primary evaluation criteria.

How Submissions Worked

Authors uploaded their abstracts through the IIT Madras SustainX Submission Portal, where entries could be saved and revised until the deadline. The portal is now closed; the steps below are kept as a record of the process.
